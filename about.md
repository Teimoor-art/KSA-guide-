# About KSA Guide

KSA Guide is an independent, informational website created to help travelers, new residents, job seekers, and pilgrims navigate life in Saudi Arabia — from visa applications and Iqama services to travel destinations and everyday practical guides.

## Our Mission

Saudi Arabia has changed rapidly over the past few years, and a lot of information available online is outdated or scattered across many different sources. Our goal is simple: bring together clear, practical, up-to-date guides in one place, written in plain language.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Visa guides** — tourist, work, family visit, student, and Umrah visas
- **Residency help** — Iqama status, Absher, exit re-entry visas, and more
- **Travel guides** — Riyadh, Jeddah, AlUla, and other destinations worth visiting
- **Living in Saudi Arabia** — cost of living, banking, SIM cards, health insurance, and everyday practical advice
- **Working in Saudi Arabia** — job market insights, salary expectations, and labor law basics like end-of-service benefits

## Independence Disclaimer

KSA Guide is an independently run informational resource. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Saudi government, any ministry, or any official visa or residency platform (including Absher, Muqeem, or Nusuk). For official transactions, always use the relevant government platform directly.
