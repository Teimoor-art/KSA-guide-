# Saudi Arabia Visit Visa 2026: Complete Guide

Planning to visit Saudi Arabia in 2026? Here is your complete step by step guide.

## 1. Types of Visit Visa
- **Tourist Visa**: For tourism and Umrah
- **Family Visit Visa**: To visit family and relatives
- **Business Visit Visa**: For business meetings and events

## 2. Required Documents
1. Passport - Valid for at least 6 months
2. Passport Size Photo - White background
3. Visa Application Form
4. Hotel Booking or Invitation Letter
5. Return Flight Ticket

## 3. How to Apply - Step by Step
**Step 1:** Go to the official KSA Visa Portal and create an account  
**Step 2:** Fill the application form and pay the visa fee online  
**Step 3:** Upload all required documents  
**Step 4:** Wait for approval. Processing time is 3-5 business days

## 4. Visa Fees 2026
Tourist Visa: ~$80 USD
Processing Time: 3-5 Working Days

## 5. Important Tips
- Keep a copy of your passport and visa
- Book your return ticket in advance
- Check visa validity before traveling
