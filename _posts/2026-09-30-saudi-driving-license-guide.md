# Saudi Driving License for Expats in 2026: Complete Guide

Getting a Saudi driving license can make daily life much easier for expatriates living in Saudi Arabia. This guide explains the general process, required documents and important things to know.

## 1. Who Can Apply?

Generally, eligible residents and expatriates can apply for a Saudi driving license if they meet the applicable requirements.

Requirements can vary depending on your residency status, age, nationality and the type of license you need.

## 2. Common Requirements

You may be asked for:

- Valid Iqama or residency document
- Valid passport
- Medical examination
- Passport-size photographs, where required
- Driving school registration
- Existing driving license, if you are applying for a replacement or conversion
- Payment of applicable government and training fees

Always check the latest requirements with the official Saudi authorities before applying.

## 3. Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Check Your Eligibility

Make sure your residency and personal documents are valid.

### Step 2: Complete the Medical Examination

Complete the required medical examination through an approved medical center.

### Step 3: Register With a Driving School

If training is required, register with an approved driving school.

### Step 4: Complete the Training

Depending on your previous driving experience and assessment, you may need practical and theoretical training.

### Step 5: Take the Required Tests

You may need to complete:

- Theory test
- Practical driving assessment
- Other required assessments

### Step 6: Pay the Applicable Fees

Pay the required license, training or related fees through the available official payment channels.

### Step 7: Receive Your License

After completing the required process successfully, your Saudi driving license can be issued according to the applicable procedure.

## 4. Can an Expat Use an International Driving License?

An international driving permit may be useful for visitors in situations where it is legally accepted.

However, residents should check the current Saudi rules regarding when a Saudi driving license is required.

## 5. Important Tips

- Keep your Iqama and passport information up to date.
- Use official Saudi government platforms for applications and payments.
- Do not pay unofficial agents for government services.
- Check the latest requirements before visiting a driving school.
- Keep copies of important documents.
- Follow Saudi traffic regulations at all times.

## 6. Final Note

Driving license requirements and procedures can change. KSA GUIDE is an independent information platform and is not a government website.

For the latest official requirements, always verify information through the relevant Saudi government authorities.
