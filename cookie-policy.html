What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them.

They can help websites remember settings, maintain basic functionality, understand general usage and provide a more consistent experience.

Similar technologies, such as local storage or pixels, may also be used for related purposes.

02

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are used for functions that are necessary for a website to operate correctly.

Examples may include remembering basic cookie preferences, maintaining security functions or supporting website navigation.

Because these technologies can be necessary for core functionality, they may not always be optional.

03

Preference Cookies

Preference technologies may remember choices you make while using KSA GUIDE.

For example, the website may remember a language selection or certain interface preferences so you do not have to select them repeatedly.

Where applicable, preference technologies can be managed through the available cookie settings.

04

Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies help website owners understand general visitor activity.

They may provide information such as which pages are visited, approximate visit duration, device type, browser information and how visitors arrive at the website.

This information can help us understand which content is useful and where the website experience can be improved.

Analytics services should be configured according to applicable privacy requirements and the choices presented to visitors.

05

Advertising Cookies

KSA GUIDE may introduce advertising services in the future to help support the website.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, personalize, measure or limit advertisements according to their systems and applicable settings.

Advertising practices may change as the website develops. This policy may therefore be updated when advertising technologies are introduced or changed.

06

Third-Party Cookies

Some features or services used by the website may be provided by third parties.

These providers may place or access their own cookies or similar technologies when their services are active.

Examples may include analytics providers, advertising platforms, embedded media, security services or other website infrastructure.

Third-party providers operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

07

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies in several ways.

Website Cookie Controls

Where available, KSA GUIDE provides controls allowing visitors to accept all cookies or reject non-essential cookies.

Browser Settings

Most modern browsers allow you to block, delete or limit cookies through their privacy settings.

Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features or preferences.

Device Settings

Mobile and desktop operating systems may also provide privacy and tracking controls that affect how websites store or access information.

08

Your Cookie Choice

When the KSA GUIDE cookie banner is displayed, you may choose between the available options.

COOKIE OPTIONS

Your choices matter.

Accept All allows optional cookies where they are enabled.

Reject Non-Essential limits optional cookies while allowing essential website functions.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.
