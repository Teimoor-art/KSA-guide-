AlUla has quickly become one of Saudi Arabia's most talked-about destinations, and for good reason — few places pack this much ancient history and dramatic desert scenery into one region.

## Hegra — Saudi Arabia's First UNESCO Site

Hegra (Mada'in Salih) is the star attraction, home to over a hundred monumental tombs carved directly into sandstone outcrops by the Nabataeans over 2,000 years ago. Guided tours are required to visit, and walking among the tombs at sunrise or sunset is widely considered the best time to go.

## Elephant Rock (Jabal AlFil)

One of AlUla's most photographed natural formations, this massive sandstone rock naturally resembles an elephant. The surrounding area has been developed with a small amphitheater and food stalls, making it a popular evening spot to watch the sunset.

## Maraya Concert Hall

Recognized as the world's largest mirrored building, Maraya reflects the surrounding desert landscape and has become a striking venue for concerts and cultural events. Even without an event scheduled, it's worth seeing from the outside.

## AlUla Old Town

Wandering AlUla's old town gives a sense of what daily life looked like here for centuries, with mudbrick buildings and narrow alleyways that have been carefully preserved and partially restored for visitors.

## Getting to AlUla

AlUla has its own airport with direct domestic and some international connections, making it far more accessible than a desert destination might suggest. It's also reachable by road from Madinah, roughly a few hours' drive.

## Best Time to Visit

The cooler months from October through March are considered the best time to visit, particularly for outdoor activities like hiking and the Hegra tombs tour, since summer temperatures in the region climb very high.

## Where to Stay

Accommodation ranges from luxury desert resorts to more affordable hotel options in AlUla town, with several properties designed to blend into the surrounding rock formations rather than compete with them visually.

## A Practical Tip

Book Hegra tour tickets in advance through the official AlUla platform, as availability is limited per group and popular time slots — especially sunrise tours — can sell out during peak season.
