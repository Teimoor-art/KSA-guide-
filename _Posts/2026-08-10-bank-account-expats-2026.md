Opening a local bank account is one of the first practical steps every new resident takes in Saudi Arabia — it's needed for salary deposits, rent payments, and everyday transactions. Here's how the process works.

## Who Can Open an Account

Any resident with a valid Iqama can open a personal bank account. Most employers actually require it, since salaries are paid directly into a local account rather than in cash.

## Documents You'll Need

- Valid Iqama (original and copy)
- Passport (original and copy)
- Proof of employment or an employment letter from your sponsor
- A Saudi mobile number registered in your name
- Sometimes a salary certificate, depending on the bank

## Choosing a Bank

Saudi Arabia has several major local and international banks, each offering slightly different account types, mobile app experiences, and fee structures. Popular choices among expats include a mix of large local banks with wide ATM networks and digital-first banks that offer fully app-based account opening.

## The Application Process

1. Visit a branch in person (some banks now offer remote account opening via their app)
2. Submit your Iqama, passport, and employment documents
3. Complete biometric verification (fingerprint scan) as required by Saudi banking regulations
4. Receive your debit card and set up online/mobile banking access

## What to Expect After Opening

- Most banks offer a mobile app for transfers, bill payments, and balance checks
- International transfers (for sending money home) are commonly used features — compare fees across banks before committing, as they vary
- Some accounts come with minimum balance requirements or monthly fees, so it's worth asking about the fee structure upfront

## A Useful Tip

If your employer has a relationship with a specific bank for payroll processing, opening an account there can sometimes speed up the process, since the bank is already familiar with your company's documentation requirements.
