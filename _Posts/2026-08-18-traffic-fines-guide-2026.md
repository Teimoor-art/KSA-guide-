Traffic violations in Saudi Arabia are tracked digitally, and both checking and paying fines is a fully online process — no need to visit a traffic department in person.

## Checking Your Fines

The easiest way to check outstanding traffic fines is through Absher:

1. Log into the Absher app or website
2. Navigate to "Traffic Violations" under vehicle or individual services
3. View a list of any recorded violations, including the fine amount and reason

Fines can also be checked through the Muroor (Traffic Department) portal or select banking apps that have integrated traffic fine lookups.

## Paying a Fine

Once you've confirmed a fine, payment can be made through:

- The Absher app directly, using a linked card or SADAD payment service
- Your bank's mobile app, using the SADAD billing number associated with the fine
- ATMs that support SADAD payments

## The Points-Based System

Saudi Arabia uses a points system alongside financial penalties for certain violations. Accumulating too many points within a set period can lead to license suspension, so repeated violations carry consequences beyond just the fine amount.

## Common Violations and Their Fine Ranges

Fine amounts vary significantly by violation type and severity:

- Minor violations, like not wearing a seatbelt, typically carry lower fines
- Speeding fines increase substantially with how far over the limit you're driving
- Serious violations, such as reckless driving, carry the highest fines and can include additional penalties like vehicle impoundment

## Discount Periods

Saudi authorities occasionally offer discount periods on outstanding fines, allowing reduced payment if settled within a specific window — it's worth checking Absher notifications or official announcements if you have older unpaid fines.

## What Happens If You Don't Pay

Unpaid traffic fines can eventually affect your ability to renew vehicle registration (istimara) or, in some cases, your Iqama renewal, so it's worth clearing fines promptly rather than letting them accumulate.

## A Practical Tip

Enable notifications in the Absher app so you're alerted as soon as a new violation is recorded — this makes it much easier to catch and pay fines early, often within any available discount window.
