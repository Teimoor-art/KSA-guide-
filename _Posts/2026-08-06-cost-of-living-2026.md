Whether you're planning to relocate for work or just curious what daily life costs in the Kingdom, here's a practical breakdown of living expenses across Saudi Arabia's major cities.

## Housing

Housing is usually the biggest line item in any budget. Riyadh and Jeddah tend to be pricier than smaller cities like Dammam or Abha, with rents varying significantly based on whether you choose a compound (popular with expat families) or a standalone apartment in the city.

- Compounds often include shared pools, gyms, and security, at a premium
- Standalone apartments are generally more affordable but vary widely by neighborhood
- Utilities (electricity, water, internet) are typically billed separately from rent

## Groceries and Food

Grocery costs are comparable to many mid-range countries, with international supermarket chains available in most cities alongside local markets that offer better prices on fresh produce. Eating out ranges from very affordable local eateries to upscale international dining, especially in Riyadh's newer entertainment districts.

## Transportation

- Fuel is notably inexpensive compared to most Western countries
- Public transport (metro and buses) is expanding rapidly in Riyadh, reducing reliance on private cars
- Ride-hailing apps are widely used and reasonably priced for short trips

## Healthcare

Employers are generally required to provide health insurance for their employees, which covers most routine and emergency care. Private clinics offer faster service for a fee if you prefer not to use your employer's designated network.

## Education

For expat families, private international schools are the standard choice, and costs vary substantially by curriculum (British, American, IB) and school reputation. This is often the second-largest expense after housing for families with children.

## The Upside: No Income Tax

One factor that significantly changes the math for expats is that personal income in Saudi Arabia is generally not taxed, which noticeably increases take-home pay compared to many other countries.

## Budgeting Tip

Before relocating, it's worth researching typical rent and school costs specifically for the city and neighborhood you're targeting, since prices between Riyadh, Jeddah, and smaller cities can differ substantially.
