# Saudi Work Visa 2026: Complete Guide

Want to work in Saudi Arabia in 2026? Here is the complete process to get a Saudi Work Visa, requirements, and important tips.

## 1. Types of Work Visa
- **Single Entry Work Visa**: For new employees
- **Multiple Entry Work Visa**: For business visits
- **Family Work Visa**: To bring family members

## 2. Required Documents
1. Passport - Minimum 1 year validity
2. Job Offer Letter from Saudi Company
3. Educational Certificates - Attested
4. Experience Certificates
5. Passport Size Photos - White background
6. Police Clearance Certificate
7. Medical Report from GAMCA approved center

## 3. Work Visa Fees 2026
- **Visa Fee**: ~2000 SAR
- **Medical Test**: ~100 USD
- **Agency Charges**: Varies by country
- **Iqama Fee**: Paid by employer after arrival

## 4. How to Apply - Step by Step
**Step 1**: Get a job offer from a Saudi company
**Step 2**: Company will issue Work Visa Authorization from Ministry of Labor
**Step 3**: Complete medical test and submit documents to embassy
**Step 4**: Visa stamping on passport within 7-10 working days
**Step 5**: Travel to KSA and get Iqama within 90 days

## 5. Important Notes
- Medical must be done from approved GAMCA centers only
- Degree attestation is mandatory
- Employer will provide accommodation and health insurance
- You cannot change jobs in first 2 years without permission

---
**Note**: Rules and fees may change. Always verify with Saudi Embassy and your employer before applying.
