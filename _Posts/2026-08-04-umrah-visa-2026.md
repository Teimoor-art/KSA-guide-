# Umrah Visa 2026: Complete Guide

Planning for Umrah in 2026? This guide covers everything you need to know about applying for an Umrah Visa, requirements, costs, and the application process.

## 1. Types of Umrah Visa
- **Single Entry Umrah Visa**: Valid for 90 days
- **Multiple Entry Umrah Visa**: Valid for 1 year
- **Transit Umrah Visa**: Valid for 96 hours

## 2. Required Documents
1. Passport - Minimum 6 months validity
2. Passport size photos - White background, 4 copies
3. Confirmed Return Flight Ticket
4. Hotel Booking in Makkah and Madinah
5. Travel Insurance

## 3. Umrah Visa Fees 2026
- **Visa Fee**: ~300 SAR
- **Health Insurance**: ~180 SAR  
- **Agency Service Charges**: Varies by country
- **Estimated Total Cost**: 800 - 1200 USD

## 4. How to Apply - Step by Step
**Step 1**: Contact a registered travel agency or apply online via Nusuk platform
**Step 2**: Submit required documents and payment
**Step 3**: Agency will process your application on KSA portal
**Step 4**: E-Visa will be issued to your email within 2-3 working days

## 5. Important Notes
- You can stay up to 90 days on Umrah Visa
- Work is not allowed on Umrah Visa
- You can visit Makkah, Madinah, and other cities in Saudi Arabia

**Note**: Fees and regulations may change. Please check the official Saudi government website before applying.
