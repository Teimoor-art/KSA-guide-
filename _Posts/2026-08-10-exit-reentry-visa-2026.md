If you're a resident planning to travel outside Saudi Arabia and return, you'll need an exit re-entry visa before you leave — without it, re-entering the country isn't possible on your existing Iqama.

## What Is an Exit Re-Entry Visa

Unlike a final exit visa (which permanently cancels your residency), the exit re-entry visa allows you to leave the Kingdom temporarily and come back while keeping your Iqama and job valid.

## Single vs. Multiple Exit Re-Entry Visas

- **Single exit re-entry** — covers one trip out and back within a specified validity period
- **Multiple exit re-entry** — allows several trips in and out during the validity period, useful for residents who travel frequently for work or family reasons

## How to Apply

1. Log into Absher or Muqeem
2. Select the exit re-entry visa service
3. Choose single or multiple entry
4. Enter your intended travel dates (or a general validity window for multiple entry)
5. Pay the applicable government fee
6. Receive approval, typically processed quickly through the online system

## Who Applies — You or Your Sponsor

In most cases, your employer or sponsor needs to approve or initiate the request through their Muqeem account, since it's linked to your work sponsorship. Some companies have delegated this to HR departments who handle it routinely for staff travel.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Applying with an Iqama that's close to expiry — the exit re-entry visa validity typically can't exceed your Iqama's remaining validity
- Choosing single entry when multiple trips are planned, resulting in needing to reapply
- Forgetting to apply before departure, which can leave you unable to return

## Overstaying Abroad

Each exit re-entry visa has a maximum duration you're allowed to stay outside the Kingdom, often up to a set number of months depending on the type issued. Overstaying this period can affect your Iqama status, so it's worth double-checking your specific visa's validity window before finalizing travel plans.

## A Practical Tip

Apply for your exit re-entry visa as soon as travel dates are confirmed rather than waiting until the last moment — while processing is usually fast, having buffer time avoids any risk of last-minute issues affecting your flight.
