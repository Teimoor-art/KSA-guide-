Absher is the backbone of digital government services in Saudi Arabia. From renewing an Iqama to booking a passport appointment, almost every official transaction now runs through this single platform. Here's how to get set up.

## What Is Absher

Absher is run by the Ministry of Interior and gives citizens, residents, and even visitors access to services like traffic violation checks, exit/re-entry visa requests, Iqama management, and family member sponsorship — all from a phone or browser.

## Registering on Absher (For Residents)

1. Download the Absher app (available for iOS and Android) or visit the Absher website
2. Select "Individuals" registration
3. Enter your Iqama number, date of birth, and mobile number registered with your sponsor
4. Verify with the OTP sent by SMS
5. Set a secure password and PIN for future logins

## Logging In

Once registered, logging in is straightforward:

1. Open the app or website
2. Enter your Iqama/ID number and password
3. Confirm with your phone's fingerprint, face ID, or an OTP

## Key Services Available on Absher

- **Iqama services** — check validity, print digital copy, view sponsor details
- **Traffic services** — pay fines, renew vehicle registration
- **Exit/re-entry visas** — request and track approval status
- **Passport services** — book appointments, track applications
- **Family services** — manage dependents' records if you're a sponsor

## Common Login Issues and Fixes

- **Forgot password:** Use the "Forgot Password" option and verify via your registered mobile number
- **OTP not arriving:** Confirm your mobile number is correctly linked in your Absher profile; SIM changes require an update at a government service center
- **Account locked:** Multiple failed attempts trigger a temporary lock — wait 30 minutes or visit an Absher help center

## A Quick Tip

Keep your registered mobile number updated at all times. Since almost every Absher action requires OTP verification, an outdated number is the most common reason residents get locked out of essential services.
