Saudi Arabia's Premium Residency program, known locally as Iqama Mumtaza, offers foreign nationals a way to live in the Kingdom without needing employer sponsorship — a significant shift from the traditional Iqama system.

## What Makes It Different From a Regular Iqama

A standard Iqama is tied to an employer or sponsor, meaning your residency status depends on your job. Premium Residency removes that dependency, allowing holders to live, work, and invest more freely without a Saudi sponsor.

## The Two Main Options

- **Permanent Premium Residency** — a one-time payment for indefinite residency status, renewable and passed down under certain conditions
- **One-Year Premium Residency** — an annual renewable option at a lower cost, suited to those who want flexibility without the larger upfront commitment

## Key Benefits

- Freedom to work for any employer, start a business, or remain unemployed without residency risk
- Ability to own property in designated areas
- No employer sponsorship required for entry, exit, or travel
- Family members can typically be sponsored as dependents under the same status

## Eligibility Requirements

Applicants generally need to meet financial and background criteria, including proof of income or assets, a clean criminal record, and passing health and security checks. Specific thresholds are set and periodically updated by the Premium Residency program authority.

## How to Apply

1. Create an account on the official Premium Residency platform
2. Submit required documents, including passport, financial proof, and background clearance
3. Pay the applicable fee for your chosen residency type
4. Await review and approval, after which your Premium Residency card is issued

## Who Typically Applies

This program tends to appeal to investors, retirees, remote professionals, and business owners who want long-term flexibility in Saudi Arabia without being tied to a single employer's sponsorship.

## A Practical Tip

Because program details and fees are periodically updated, it's worth checking the official Premium Residency platform directly for the most current requirements before starting your application, rather than relying solely on older guides or secondhand information.
