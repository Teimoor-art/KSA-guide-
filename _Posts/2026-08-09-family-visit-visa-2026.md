If you have relatives living and working in Saudi Arabia, a family visit visa lets you stay with them for an extended period without needing a tourist e-Visa. Here's how the process works.

## Who Can Sponsor a Family Visit Visa

Any Saudi resident (expat or citizen) with a valid Iqama can sponsor immediate family members — typically parents, spouse, children, and siblings — for a visit visa through the Absher or Muqeem platform.

## What You'll Need

- A copy of the sponsor's Iqama and passport
- The visitor's passport, valid for at least six months
- Proof of relationship (marriage certificate, birth certificate, or similar, depending on the relation)
- A recent passport-style photo of the visitor

## How the Application Works

1. The sponsor logs into Absher or Muqeem and selects the family visit visa service
2. Relationship details and visitor information are entered
3. Required documents are uploaded
4. The application is submitted for review by the Ministry of Interior
5. Once approved, the visitor applies for the actual visa stamp through the relevant channel, often linked to the approval

## Visa Duration

Family visit visas are typically issued for stays ranging from a few weeks up to 90 days per visit, with some multiple-entry options available depending on the sponsor's status and history.

## Common Reasons for Delay or Rejection

- Missing or unclear relationship documentation
- Sponsor's Iqama nearing expiry at the time of application
- Incomplete visitor passport information

## A Practical Tip

Start the sponsorship request at least three to four weeks before the intended travel date. While some approvals come faster, government processing times can vary, and having a buffer avoids last-minute travel stress.
