Saudi Arabia's job market has changed dramatically over the past few years, driven largely by Vision 2030's push into tourism, entertainment, technology, and giga-projects. Here's what job seekers should know.

## Sectors With the Most Demand

- **Construction and engineering** — driven by giga-projects like NEOM, Qiddiya, and The Red Sea Project
- **Healthcare** — ongoing demand for doctors, nurses, and allied health professionals across public and private hospitals
- **Technology** — a fast-growing sector as the Kingdom invests heavily in digital transformation and smart cities
- **Hospitality and tourism** — expanding rapidly alongside new resorts, entertainment venues, and international events
- **Education** — international schools and universities regularly hire foreign teachers and administrators

## How Salaries Typically Work

Salary packages in Saudi Arabia are often structured differently than in Western countries. A typical offer may include:

- A base monthly salary
- A housing allowance (or company-provided accommodation)
- A transportation allowance
- Annual flight tickets home
- End-of-service benefits, calculated based on years worked

## The Tax-Free Advantage

One of the most attractive features for expats is that personal income is generally not taxed in Saudi Arabia. This means the salary figure quoted in a job offer is typically very close to actual take-home pay, which can make offers more competitive than they first appear compared to countries with high income tax.

## Saudization and What It Means for Job Seekers

The Saudization program (Nitaqat) requires companies to hire a certain percentage of Saudi nationals depending on company size and sector. This affects which roles are open to expats — highly specialized or technical positions tend to remain more accessible to foreign hires, while certain entry-level roles are increasingly reserved for locals.

## How to Find Jobs

- Company websites and LinkedIn remain the most common channels for professional roles
- Recruitment agencies specializing in Gulf placements handle a significant share of engineering, healthcare, and construction hiring
- Government job portals like Qiwa also list opportunities and are used by employers to process work permits

## A Realistic Expectation

Salaries vary enormously by industry, seniority, and nationality of the hiring company (multinational vs. local firms often differ). Researching typical ranges for your specific role and industry — rather than relying on general averages — will give a far more accurate picture before you negotiate an offer.
