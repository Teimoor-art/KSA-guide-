If you're living and working in Saudi Arabia, your Iqama — the residency permit — is the single most important document you carry. Knowing how to check its status online saves you from unexpected fines or travel disruptions.

## What Is an Iqama

The Iqama is issued to every foreign resident working or living in Saudi Arabia. It confirms your legal residency status, links to your employer or sponsor, and must be renewed periodically — usually annually, though terms vary by employment contract.

## Checking Your Iqama Status Online

There are two main official platforms residents use:

### 1. Muqeem Portal
Muqeem is the Ministry of Interior's dedicated platform for residency services. To check your status:

1. Go to the Muqeem website
2. Log in using your Iqama number and the registered mobile number
3. Enter the OTP sent to your phone
4. Your dashboard will show Iqama validity, expiry date, and any pending actions

### 2. Absher Individuals
Absher is the broader government services app that also displays Iqama information:

1. Open the Absher app or website
2. Log in with your national ID / Iqama number and password
3. Navigate to "My Services" then "Iqama Information"
4. View expiry date, sponsor details, and renewal status

## What to Do If Your Iqama Is Expiring Soon

Renewal is typically handled by your employer or sponsor through the Muqeem or Qiwa platform, but it's wise to confirm the process has started at least a month before expiry. An expired Iqama can result in fines and travel restrictions.

## Checking Iqama Status Without Login

Some residents prefer a quick check without logging in. The Absher "Public Services" section allows verification of Iqama validity using just the Iqama number and date of birth — useful if you've forgotten your password.

## Why This Matters

An invalid or expired Iqama affects far more than paperwork — it can block bank transactions, SIM card renewals, and even exit/re-entry visa processing. Set a calendar reminder a month ahead of your expiry date to stay safe.
