Applying for a Saudi Arabia tourist visa has never been simpler. Since the Kingdom opened its doors to international tourism, most nationalities can now apply for an e-Visa entirely online — no embassy visit required. Here's exactly how the process works in 2026.

## Who Can Apply

Citizens of more than 65 countries are eligible for the Saudi tourist e-Visa, including most of Europe, North America, and several Asian countries. The visa allows entry for tourism, family visits, and Umrah (outside peak religious seasons).

## What You'll Need

- A passport valid for at least six months from your entry date
- A recent digital passport-style photo
- A valid debit or credit card for the visa fee
- Confirmed return or onward travel details (recommended, not always required)

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official Saudi e-Visa portal or the Visit Saudi app
2. Create an account and fill in your passport and personal details
3. Upload your photo and passport bio page
4. Pay the visa fee online
5. Receive your e-Visa by email, usually within minutes to a few days

## Visa Validity and Stay Duration

The tourist e-Visa is typically valid for one year from the date of issue and allows multiple entries. Each visit can last up to 90 days, though the total stay within the validity period is capped — check the current limit at the time of your application, as rules are occasionally updated.

## What the Visa Includes

The e-Visa also comes bundled with travel insurance covering COVID-19 and basic medical emergencies during your stay, which is a detail many first-time visitors don't expect.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Applying with a passport that has less than six months validity
- Uploading a blurry or non-compliant photo, which delays approval
- Assuming the tourist visa covers Hajj — it does not; Hajj requires a separate, government-allocated visa

## Final Tip

Apply at least a week before your planned travel dates, even though approval is often fast. This gives you buffer time in case additional document verification is requested.
