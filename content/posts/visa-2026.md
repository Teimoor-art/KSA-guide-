# Saudi Arabia Visit Visa 2026

Want to travel to Saudi Arabia? Here is the complete guide to apply for a visit visa in 2026.

## 1. Types of Visit Visas
- **Tourist Visa**: Valid for 90 days
- **Family Visit Visa**: To meet relatives in Saudi Arabia
- **Umrah Visa**: For performing Umrah

## 2. How to Apply
1. Go to the official website `visa.mofa.gov.sa`
2. Create an account and fill the application form
3. Pay the visa fees online
4. Wait for approval
