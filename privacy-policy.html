01

Overview

KSA GUIDE is an independent Saudi Arabia information, travel and digital services platform. We respect the privacy of people who visit and use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains the general types of information that may be collected when you visit ksa-guide.com and how that information may be used.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for operating, improving and communicating through the website.

Information you provide

If you contact us, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, message contents and other information you choose to include.

Technical information

Like many websites, technical information may be processed automatically by hosting, security, analytics or other website infrastructure. This can include browser type, device information, approximate location derived from technical data, referring pages and basic usage information.

Location permission

If a website feature requests access to your device location, the browser should ask for permission. KSA GUIDE does not require you to grant location permission to browse the website.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to messages and enquiries.
  • Providing requested digital services.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.
  • Understanding general website usage.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing abuse.
  • Managing website preferences such as cookie settings.

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of the website or the communication you requested without an appropriate reason or applicable legal basis.

04

Cookies

KSA GUIDE may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality and, where enabled, understand website usage and preferences.

Essential cookies may be necessary for features of the website to work correctly.

Optional analytics or preference cookies may be controlled through the website's cookie settings where those controls are available.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Analytics

We may use analytics technologies to understand how visitors interact with the website, which pages are useful and how the website can be improved.

Analytics services may process technical information such as page views, device type, browser information, approximate geographic information and referral information.

Analytics should be configured in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and the choices presented to visitors.

06

Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party providers for services such as hosting, fonts, analytics, security, embedded content, forms, advertising or other technical functions.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

External websites linked from KSA GUIDE are not controlled by us. You should review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

Advertising

KSA GUIDE may use advertising services in the future, including services such as Google AdSense, to support the operation of the website.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable settings.

If advertising is introduced, this policy and the relevant cookie information may be updated to reflect the services actually in use.

08

Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information handled through the website.

However, no internet transmission, website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary website contact channels unless specifically requested and appropriate safeguards are available.

09

Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection in certain circumstances.

You may also:

  • Choose whether to allow optional cookies where controls are provided.
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings.
  • Contact us regarding information you have voluntarily submitted.
  • Stop using the website at any time.

Requests can be sent using the contact details below.
