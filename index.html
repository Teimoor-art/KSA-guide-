Practical Information

Travel Guide

Essential information to plan your journey. Always verify official sources before travel.

Visa Information

Tourist eVisa, Umrah, family visit and other entry options. Rules change — check official portals.

Verify: visitsaudi.com / moi.gov.sa

Entry Requirements

Passport validity, health requirements and immigration procedures at Saudi airports.

Official sources recommended

Airports

King Khalid (RUH), King Abdulaziz (JED), King Fahd (DMM) and other international gateways.

Transportation

Domestic flights, SAPTCO buses, ride-hailing apps, car rental and intercity options.

SIM & Internet

STC, Mobily and Zain tourist SIMs available at airports and stores.

Currency & Payments

Saudi Riyal (SAR). Cards widely accepted; cash useful in markets and small shops.

Weather & Best Time

Cooler season October–April is ideal for most regions. Summers are hot inland.

Local Customs & Dress

Modest clothing recommended. Respect local traditions, especially in religious sites.

Emergency Numbers

Police 999 · Ambulance 997 · Civil Defense 998 · Traffic 993

Heritage

Culture & Heritage

Saudi hospitality, traditions, architecture and living culture across the regions.

Saudi Hospitality

Warm welcomes, Arabic coffee and dates are the heart of Saudi hospitality. Guests are treated with generosity and respect.

Traditional Clothing

Thobe for men and abaya for women remain elegant everyday attire, with regional variations in style and fabric.

Saudi Coffee & Dates

Gahwa served with dates is a ritual of welcome. Each region has its preferred roast and cardamom blend.

Historical Sites

From Hegra and Diriyah to Al-Balad and the Rock Art of Hail — Saudi Arabia’s archaeological treasures span millennia.

Traditional Markets

Souqs offer spices, incense, textiles, jewellery and local crafts. Experience bargaining and authentic atmosphere.

Festivals & Events

Riyadh Season, Jeddah Season, AlUla events and National Day celebrations showcase modern Saudi culture.

Cuisine

Saudi Food

Discover the flavours of the Kingdom — from Kabsa to Mandi and traditional desserts.

🍚

Kabsa

National rice dish with meat and fragrant spices.

🍖

Mandi

Slow-cooked meat and rice with Yemeni roots, popular across KSA.

🥣

Jareesh

Crushed wheat porridge with meat — comfort food of the central region.

🥛

Saleeg

Creamy rice and milk dish from the Hejaz region.

🥟

Mutabbaq

Stuffed flatbread with egg, meat or sweet fillings.

☕

Saudi Coffee

Lightly roasted with cardamom — served with dates.

🌴

Dates

Ajwa, Sukkari, Khalas and many more varieties.
