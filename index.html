Official Guides & Links

Iqama, Absher, visas, fees and government platforms. Always verify the latest information on the official websites.

📱

Absher

Official Ministry of Interior platform for citizens, residents and visitors. Iqama, traffic, passports, appointments and more.

Open Absher →
🪪

Iqama Renewal

Residence permit renewal. Typical base fee \~ SAR 650/year (employer usually pays). Always confirm current amount.

Work permit levy: approx. SAR 700–800 / month (tier dependent)

Check on Absher →
👨‍👩‍👧

Dependent Levy

SAR 400 per dependent per month (SAR 4,800 / year per person). Paid via Absher / Muqeem.

Verify exact amount before payment – fees can be updated.

Pay / Check Absher →
✈️

Exit / Re-Entry Visa

Single & multiple exit/re-entry visas issued through Absher / Muqeem. Common reference fees: around SAR 200 (single) / SAR 500 (multiple) – always verify live.

Issue on Absher →
💼

Qiwa

Ministry of Human Resources platform for work contracts, Saudization, work permits and labor services.

Open Qiwa →
🏛️

MISA & Visit Saudi

Investment (MISA) and official tourism / eVisa information.

MISA → Visit Saudi →
